About

0xFF-MinusOne is an independent white-hat initiative. Its concept is vulnerability response that does not depend on private companies. It is not a company and does not work on behalf of companies: volunteers find vulnerabilities, report them privately to the developers and administrators who can fix them, and build the tools needed to do that work.

  1. Independent. Not a company, and not part of any security vendor.
  2. Sensitive details stay local. Only what is needed to track progress goes to the cloud.
  3. Report privately first. To the developers and administrators who can fix the problem.
  4. Public information and authorized scope only. We never break in without permission.

Send questions and corrections to Issues in each repository.
